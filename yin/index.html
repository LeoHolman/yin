Yin is designed as an introductory tool to help beginning learners of Chinese understand, recognize, and produce Chinese tone. As a foundation of the Chinese language, tone is important to effective communication. Experienced users can jump to a lesson or activity through the tap at the top of the page, but we recommend first time users press the button below to enter the first lesson and start from the beginning.
